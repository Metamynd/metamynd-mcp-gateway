# Security policy

This gateway sits in the path of privileged actions and holds your tools' credentials. We would much rather hear about
a weakness from you than from an incident.

## Reporting

**Do not open a public issue for a security report.**

Use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Metamynd/metamynd-mcp-gateway/security/advisories/new)
on this repository, or email **security@metamynd.ai**.

Include what you have: the version, what you did, what happened, and what you expected. A rough report is more useful
to us than a polished one you never send.
